# Refund and Cancellation Policy

**Document owner:** Finance Operations
**Last reviewed:** 2026-01-15
**Applies to:** All Northwind Analytics subscription customers

## Scope

This policy governs refunds and cancellations for Northwind Analytics subscription plans. It does not
cover service credits issued under the Enterprise Service Level Agreement, which are a separate remedy
described in that document.

## Refund windows by plan

| Plan | Refund window | Approval required |
|---|---|---|
| Enterprise | 30 days from invoice date | Account manager |
| Business | 14 days from invoice date | Support lead |
| Starter | Non-refundable | N/A |

Enterprise customers may request a refund within 30 days of the invoice date. Requests submitted after
the 30-day window are not eligible for a refund, regardless of usage level. The account manager assigned
to the contract must approve every Enterprise refund before Finance processes it.

Business plan customers have a 14-day window from the invoice date. Starter plan subscriptions are
billed monthly and are not refundable; customers on Starter may cancel at any time to stop future
billing.

## How refunds are calculated

Approved refunds are prorated against unused subscription time, measured in whole days from the date the
request is received. Northwind does not refund partial days.

The following charges are never refundable, on any plan:

- Professional services and onboarding engagements that have already been delivered
- API overage charges for requests already served
- Third-party marketplace fees
- Custom development work accepted by the customer

## Cancellation

Customers may cancel at any time from the billing settings page or by contacting their account manager.
Cancellation stops renewal at the end of the current billing period; it does not by itself trigger a
refund. To request both a cancellation and a refund, the customer must submit the refund request
separately within the applicable window above.

Annual contracts cancelled mid-term remain payable for the committed term unless the contract includes
a termination-for-convenience clause.

## Processing time

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 10 business days. Customers paying
by invoice receive a credit note applied to the next invoice, or a bank transfer if no future invoices
are expected.

## Disputes

If a customer disputes a charge with their card issuer before contacting Northwind support, the account
is suspended pending resolution. We ask customers to contact billing@northwind-analytics.example first.
